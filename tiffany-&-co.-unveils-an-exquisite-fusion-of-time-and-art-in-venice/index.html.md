# Why Tiffany & Co.'s Venice Unveiling Is More Than Just Jewelry...

Tiffany & Co. (@tiffanyandco) has once again pushed the boundaries of luxury and craftsmanship with its latest watch collection, unveiled in the romantic city of Venice. The limited series, Eternity by Tiffany, is a breathtaking testament to the brand's legacy in watchmaking and diamond artistry, masterfully fused with the age-old tradition of Venetian glassmaking.

## A Collaboration Steeped in History and Craft

At the heart of this extraordinary creation is a collaboration with Venini, the renowned artistic glass Maison founded in Murano in 1921. This partnership results in a stunning union of color, craft, and artistry.

Each watch in the collection features a bespoke Venini glass dial, each piece unique, adorned with 12 individually hand-set aquamarines. These exquisite touches are beautifully complemented by a full pavé diamond bracelet, amassing nearly 5 carats. The result is a dazzling display of craftsmanship and elegance.

## The Art of Murano Glassmaking

The artistry of Murano glass shines in the design of the dials. Crafting the distinctive glass face is a meticulous process, requiring skill and precision to achieve the striking effect. The swirling blue hues evoke the iconic Tiffany Blue, adding an element of serene luxury.

Creating these timepieces involves an intricate assembly process. Each aquamarine is carefully placed by hand, ensuring that every watch is a singular masterpiece. The sapphire blue tones contrast beautifully against the backdrop of shimmering diamonds.
