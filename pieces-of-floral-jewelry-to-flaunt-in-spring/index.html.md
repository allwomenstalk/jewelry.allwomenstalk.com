# 21 Stunning Floral Jewelry Pieces to Refresh Your Spring Wardrobe

Spring is just around the corner, and it’s the perfect season to refresh your style! As warm weather arrives, lighter layers mean your accessories take center stage. Whether you love subtle botanical accents or show-stopping bloom motifs, adding floral jewelry is one of the easiest ways to elevate your outfits. Here are 21 stunning floral jewelry pieces to inspire your spring wardrobe refresh.

Here are three quick styling tips to keep in mind when wearing floral jewelry:

- **Balance the Scale:** Pair bold statement pieces with simple necklines, and let delicate studs shine with casual tees.

- **Mix Textures:** Combine enamel petals, sparkling crystals, and metallic vines for depth.

- **Highlight Key Hues:** Pick one accent color from your jewelry to match your handbag or shoes.
