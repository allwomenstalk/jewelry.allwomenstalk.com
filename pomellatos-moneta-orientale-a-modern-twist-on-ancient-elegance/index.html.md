# The Story Behind Pomellato’s Moneta Orientale...

Jewelry has always been a reflection of culture, history, and personal expression. Pomellato [@pomellato](https://www.instagram.com/pomellato) captures this essence beautifully in their latest addition to the Memorie d’Archivio 2026 capsule collection – the Moneta Orientale. This stunning piece reimagines an ancient talisman with a contemporary flair, intertwining the past and present harmoniously.

## A Revival of History

The Moneta Orientale is a reimagined version of a minimal matte yellow gold coin pendant from 1997, revived for modern jewelry lovers. At its core, this piece is about transformation and carrying the spirit of good luck into today's world. Pomellato skillfully merges historical significance with modern aesthetics, appealing to lovers of both tradition and innovation.

## The Art of Craftsmanship

This collection is a masterpiece of craftsmanship, featuring the Moneta Orientale in 33 stone-and-pavé combinations across two sizes, reflecting Pomellato’s dedication to diversity and individual style. Whether you prefer the striking contrast of turquoise and black jade against warm rose gold or the sparkle of irregular diamond pavé, there’s a unique piece for everyone.

## A Talisman for Today

Elements like turquoise, black jade, and rose gold enhance the Moneta Orientale’s allure, transforming it into a contemporary talisman. The rich hues and textures blend seamlessly, making it not just a piece of jewelry, but a statement of personal history and style. Pomellato’s attention to detail and commitment to quality bring this ancient symbol of luck into a new era, captivating the hearts of jewelry aficionados everywhere.

## Discover the Collection

The Memorie d’Archivio 2026 collection is more than just jewelry; it’s a narrative of cultural legacy and modern elegance. To explore this exquisite collection and learn more about Pomellato’s creations, visit Pomellato’s Instagram page [@pomellato](https://www.instagram.com/pomellato).

Each piece tells a story, and the Moneta Orientale invites you to become a part of its enduring legacy. Discover how this blend of ancient and contemporary design can add a touch of luck and history to your personal collection.
